Use case: stylized-concept.
Create a finished, high quality environment-and-obstacle concept art board for the medieval party platformer PAWN RUSH. This is NEW concept art, not a screenshot and not a technical diagram.
FORMAT: very large portrait 2:3 image. EXACTLY FIVE separated scene panels: one full-width panoramic panel across the upper third, two equal panels in the middle row, two equal panels in the bottom row. Thin warm ivory gutters. Narrow ivory title band at top; narrow ivory caption bands beneath each panel. Fill the canvas, five scenes only. Korean labels must be crisp and correct; no other text.
VISUAL STYLE: polished stylized 3D game art, charming faceted forms, rich but readable materials, warm cream limestone and chunky oak/brass/iron machinery. The supplied visual direction is an inviting medieval castle race with deep vertical atriums, gaps, elevated traversable walkways, red/blue heraldic banners. Keep obstacles substantial and mechanically believable with visible hinges, rails, pivots and supports. Every panel must show an obvious entry ledge in the foreground, a main obstacle crossing in the middle, and a reachable exit doorway/ledge in the background. No uniformly repeated generic wooden bridge in every scene.
CHARACTERS: 2 or 3 small cute ivory pawn adventurers per scene, spherical subtly faceted heads, simple flared pawn-shaped bodies, short black mitten hands and feet, blue or red collar. Most seen from behind or three-quarter view. Use them as scale and to demonstrate waiting, jumping, or riding. They are around 1m tall; ordinary jump gaps are small (~1–1.5 pawn heights), giant shafts are crossed by moving devices. No realistic humans.
READABILITY: obstacle mechanism is the focal point, foreground unobscured, camera elevated three-quarter view wide enough to see route. One small elegant pale amber movement arrow near each main device is allowed, no UI overlays, no dense diagrams, no paragraphs. Warm high detail, clear silhouettes. Avoid photorealism, sci-fi, modern plastic, gore, random floating structures, huge impossible jumps, invisible support machinery, watermarks.
