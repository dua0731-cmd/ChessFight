## The room: "F - Clockwork Ballroom" (stage 2, lower palace, indoors)

Game: wind-up clockwork dancer pawns stand on short wooden step lanes. Every few seconds one dancer does a quick two-square dance step toward the entrance, pauses for a moment, then glides back. The player pawns must dive into a dancer exactly while it has just stepped (en passant!) to knock it over. Knocked-over dancers fly onto a long velvet bench and lie there.
